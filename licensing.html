Licensing

The licence
is the product.

Streaming services sell a personal listening account. Playing that account where customers can hear it is a public performance — and it's the reason businesses receive invoices from rights societies they've never dealt with.

§ 01

Public performance is included

Every composition, recording and visual in the library is original work we own outright. Your subscription grants the right to play it publicly inside your facility — treatment rooms, waiting areas, hallways, retail floor.

§ 02

A certificate for every location

Each licensed address gets a dated, signed certificate in your dashboard. Download it, print it, hand it to whoever asks. Add a second site and a second certificate issues with it.

§ 03

We defend the claim, not you

If anyone brings a music-rights claim against your business over content played through SolHarmonic, we take it on and cover the defence. That indemnity is written into the agreement, not promised on a marketing page.

§ 04

Nothing about your customers leaves the room

The app plays audio and geometry. It collects no personal information, connects to no practice-management system, and stores no recordings.

§ 05

Priced per location

Not per listener, per hour, or per play. A busy day and a quiet one cost the same.

§ 06

The licence ends when you do

Cancel and playback stops; nothing is clawed back and no claim attaches to the period you were covered.

Sample language shown for layout. Final terms, and the indemnity in particular, to be drafted with IP counsel before launch. Nothing on this page is legal advice.
