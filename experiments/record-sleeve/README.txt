SOLHARMIC — LOCAL EDITION

GET STARTED
1. Extract this entire ZIP onto your computer (for example Documents/SolHarmic).
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Add your audio, or click Open music folder and select a folder containing music.

YOUR MUSIC
The included Music folder is a convenient place for your tracks. You may use any
folder on your computer. Folder selection requires a supporting browser; Chrome
or Edge is recommended. Add your audio works as a fallback.
Music is read directly from your device. Nothing is uploaded. Your browser asks
you to select files/folders again when you reopen the page. New files are not
watched automatically: select the folder again or add the new files directly.
No audio recordings are included in this download.

OPTIONAL LOCAL SERVER
If your browser restricts local fonts or files, install Python 3 and run:
  python3 start.py  (macOS / Linux)
  py start.py       (Windows)
This opens a localhost-only server and your browser. Keep its terminal open;
press Ctrl+C to stop it. It is not accessible to other computers on your network.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
index.html — page markup
styles.css — appearance and responsive layouts
player.js — playback, queue, folder picker and preferences
assets/ — seven landscapes, icons, logo, and licensed Cinzel Regular font
Music/ — optional location for your own audio

The background landscapes are static. The Waves toggle controls only the
small decorative waves over the record while audio plays.
Record sleeve, Classic/Scenic views, Solar/Lunar themes, looping, seek, volume,
queue navigation and the sleep timer remain available.

No build tools, account, cloud services or internet connection are required.
This local copy is independent of the published site. Editing one does not
update the other. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
The font license is included under assets/fonts/.
